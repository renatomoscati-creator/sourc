# HelioƧwitch

HelioƧwitch offers an innovative cloud-based service to support optimal dispatching of demand and generation flexibility via energy markets probabilistic modeling and weather forecast uncertainty propagation. The balancing in power systems is particularly difficult due to the high number of uncertainties (e.g. uncertainties in electricity consumption, weather forecast uncertainties, etc.). Taking the impact of these uncertainties into account requires an approach capable of handling them. That’s the target of HelioƧwitch.
