# Fili Pari

Fili Pari is an italian Brand focused on the development of innovative materials in textile industry, respecting the territory and the environment. Partnership with specialized companies have allowed the creation of a synergy between the Italian stone district and the textile sector, activating a new production chain. Through an analysis of the problems, combined with research and innovation, we developed cutting-edge technologies for the enhancement of marble powders. The resulting product is Veromarmo, a patented wearable microfilm containing marble powder, which promotes an efficient use of marble powder. The membrane is suitable for interior and fashion, and it could be extended in shoes sector, nautical and other different specific area. The passion for the territory and nature is reflected into the desire to value the material through sustainable development, selecting raw materials in accordance with the environment. Thanks to constant research and the increasingly advanced technology, even waste marbles become raw materials. Fili Pari promotes Veromarmo both through third party selling of the material and also with their rainwear private label.
