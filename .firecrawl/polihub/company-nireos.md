# Nireos

NIREOS is a spin-off company of the Physics Department of Politecnico di Milano, with headquarters located in Milan (Italy). The core business of NIREOS is represented by the development of groundbreaking technologies in photonics and spectroscopy. NIREOS’ portfolio includes novel and patented devices, such as broadband spectrometers and hyperspectral cameras. NIREOS’ main customers are represented by scientific research labs as well as R&D departments of industries, which exploit the power of spectroscopic analysis to perform accurate and non-destructive quality control processes.
