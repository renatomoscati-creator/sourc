# Isaac

ISAAC is an Italian startup based in Milan. Active since 2018, it is proposed as a player in the construction sector for the supply of smart technologies for seismic protection and structures monitoring. Specialized in the development of smart solutions for seismic protection and structural monitoring, ISAAC has I-Pro 1 patented: the first self-adaptive, plug-and play and one-fit-all-for-seismic protection technology, applicable to existing buildings. Recognized by Wired among the 10 Italian startups to keep an eye on in 2020, ISAAC boasts important partnerships with technological research and innovation players. ISAAC aims to revolutionize the anti-seismic practice by promoting the prevention culture and made accessible the possibility of protecting the built environment to everyone and in any context thanks to non-invasive and simple to install technologies
