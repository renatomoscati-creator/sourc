# Narvalo

Narvalo is a startup and Spin-Off Politecnico di Milano that designs and produces devices and services to improve something that surrounds us all but think little about, the air we breathe. Our first product lineup consists of the Narvalo Urban Mask and the smart add-on ‘Active Shield’ (Italian patent pending technology), are masks designed to protect you from air pollution in urban areas combining comfort, protection, performance, and sustainability
