# Creandum VC: European AI Infrastructure Startup Scout
## Target: 2-3 Pre-Seed/Seed Stage Developer & AI Infrastructure Startups

**Date:** April 15, 2026  
**Scout Report Focus:** Inference optimization, GPU orchestration, model deployment, LLM observability, AI agent frameworks, data pipeline tooling

---

## PRIMARY CANDIDATES (Ranked by Fit)

### 1. FlexAI
- **HQ:** Paris, France
- **Founded:** 2023
- **Founders:** Brijesh Tripathi (CEO, ex-Nvidia Senior Design Engineer, ex-Apple/Tesla/Zoox/Intel AXG VP), Xavier Amatriain
- **What they do:** Universal AI compute infrastructure platform providing multi-cloud orchestration for training and inference. Offers on-demand cloud services abstracting compute complexity across different accelerators (GPUs, TPUs, custom hardware).
- **Funding:** €28.5M ($30M) seed round (April 2024), led by Alpha Intelligence Capital, Eleia Partners, Heartcore Capital
- **Traction signals:** Exited stealth April 2024 with $30M seed from tier-1 VCs; co-founder ex-Nvidia with deep GPU orchestration expertise; solving immediate pain point of multi-cloud AI infrastructure fragmentation
- **Why Creandum:** Perfect picks-and-shovels play—software layer enabling seamless GPU utilization across clouds. Technical pedigree (Nvidia + Tesla) mirrors Hosted.ai's hardware-aware approach. Still in seed stage, scalable to Series A.
- **Source:** [TechCrunch: French startup FlexAI exits stealth with $30M](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/23/french-startup-flexai-exits-stealth-with-30m-to-ease-access-to-ai-compute/)

---

### 2. Linkup
- **HQ:** Paris, France
- **Founded:** 2024
- **Founders:** Philippe Mizrahi, Denis Charrier, Boris Toledano (ex-Spotify, Lyft, McKinsey)
- **What they do:** AI data pipeline infrastructure providing fast, ethical, real-time web access for LLM context and training. Solves RAG data freshness problem by enabling models to access live online content securely.
- **Funding:** €3M seed (November 2024), led by Seedcamp, Axeleo Capital, Motier Ventures
- **Traction signals:** Young but well-backed; founders from hyper-scale companies; addresses critical bottleneck in LLM apps (stale context); ethical data access differentiates from raw scraping
- **Why Creandum:** Essential data infrastructure for the AI pipeline. Fills gap between model deployment and real-world data needs. European team, early stage, strong founder pedigree in scale.
- **Source:** [EU-Startups: Paris-based Linkup raises €3 million](https://www.eu-startups.com/2024/11/paris-based-linkup-raises-e3-million-aiming-to-revolutionise-ethical-and-efficient-web-access-for-ai/)

---

### 3. DataCrunch
- **HQ:** Helsinki, Finland
- **Founded:** 2020
- **Founders:** Ruben Bryon (CEO)
- **What they do:** High-performance GPU cloud platform specializing in AI workloads. Offers on-demand GPU clusters, serverless inference, auto-scaling containers, and model hosting, powered by 100% renewable Nordic data centers.
- **Funding:** €55M Series A (September 2025), total €76.5M raised; bootstrapped to scale then raised growth capital
- **Traction signals:** €83.9M revenue (2023-2024 combined); 380% YoY growth in AI cloud revenue; already generating meaningful ARR; enterprise traction; sustainability angle differentiates
- **Why Creandum:** More mature than ideal (approaching Series B), but excellent traction and clear unit economics. If exploring growth-stage infrastructure, strong candidate. However, slightly outside pre-seed/seed mandate.
- **Source:** [EU-Startups: Finland's DataCrunch raises €55 million](https://www.eu-startups.com/2025/09/finlands-datacrunch-raises-e55-million-to-challenge-us-hyperscalers-and-accelerate-europes-ai-sovereignty/)

## SECONDARY CANDIDATES (Adjacent Infrastructure Plays)

### 4. Kestra
- **HQ:** Paris, France
- **Founded:** ~2022
- **Founders:** Not specified in sources, but open-source co-creators
- **What they do:** Enterprise orchestration platform for data, AI, and business workflows. Unifies pipeline orchestration (like Airflow) with built-in AI/ML capabilities. Open-source core with commercial platform.
- **Funding:** €25M Series A (March 2026, recently announced); strong growth metrics
- **Traction signals:** 2B workflows executed in 2025 (20x YoY growth); enterprise customers: Apple (AI pipelines), JPMorgan (cybersecurity), Toyota (monitoring), Deutsche Telekom, BHP, Crédit Agricole; 25x enterprise revenue growth since seed
- **Why Creandum:** Excellent orchestration layer for AI pipelines, but may be past seed stage for current mandate. Strong open-source traction and enterprise adoption. Monitor for future rounds.
- **Source:** [EU-Startups: Paris-based Kestra lands €21 million Series A](https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/03/paris-based-kestra-lands-e21-million-series-a-for-orchestration-platform-expansion/)

---

### 5. Lumai (Optical Computing Infrastructure)
- **HQ:** Oxford, United Kingdom
- **Founded:** 2022
- **Founders:** Xianxin Guo, James Spall, Prof. Alex Lvovsky (University of Oxford spinout)
- **What they do:** Optical computing hardware and software stack for AI acceleration. Leverages 3D optical processing to boost data center AI performance while reducing energy consumption and costs. Hardware-software co-design approach.
- **Funding:** €10M+ Series A (April 2025), led by IP Group, Constructor Capital, PhotonVentures, LIFTT
- **Traction signals:** University spinout with deep physics pedigree; hardware differentiation; energy efficiency is major market driver; early optical computing traction
- **Why Creandum:** Bold hardware approach vs. software-only plays. Aligned with "picks-and-shovels" but more hardware-focused than Cast AI profile. Worth watching as optical computing matures, but may be too early-stage hardware risk.
- **Source:** [Tech.eu: Lumai secures over $10M investment for optical processing tech](https://tech.eu/2025/04/02/lumai-secures-over-10m-investment-for-optical-processing-tech/)

## ECOSYSTEM NOTES

**Series A or Beyond (Outside Current Mandate):**
- **Nscale** (London): €958M Series B (Sept 2025) - full-stack AI cloud too mature
- **Kestra** (Paris): €25M Series A (March 2026) - past seed stage
- **DataCrunch** (Helsinki): €55M Series A with revenue - growth/scale stage

**Missing from European Seed Landscape:**
- **LLM Observability:** TensorZero (NYC, not EU) is leading; no clear European equivalent at seed stage
- **Model Fine-Tuning Infrastructure:** Gap in European market; Ray/Anyscale (US) dominate
- **Vector DBs:** Mostly US-founded (Pinecone, Weaviate leadership); European variants scarce
- **Agent Frameworks:** Not seeing European seed-stage players vs. US dominance

## RECOMMENDATION

**Top Pick for Creandum:** **FlexAI**  
- Perfect stage (seed, 2024-founded fits timeline)
- Exceptional founder pedigree (Nvidia GPU expertise)
- Clear market validation (€30M seed from strong VCs)
- Picks-and-shovels positioning (orchestration layer, not models)
- Strong software layer differentiator
- Solves real developer pain point (multi-cloud GPU management)

**Second Pick:** **Linkup**  
- Younger but cleaner founder team
- Solves critical data pipeline bottleneck for LLM inference
- Data infrastructure is underinvested vs. compute
- Early traction backing despite 2024 founding

**Alternative (if Creandum willing to stretch to Series A):** **Kestra**  
- Exceptional traction and growth metrics
- Enterprise adoption signals product-market fit
- Open-source ecosystem moat
