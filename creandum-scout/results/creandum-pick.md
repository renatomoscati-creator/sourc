## Pick: Procure AI

**One-line:** AI-native autonomous procurement platform with 4× revenue growth, named enterprise customers (EnBW, Kärcher), and a McKinsey/LBS founding team.

### 200-Word Investment Memo

Procure AI is building the AI-native operating system for enterprise procurement — autonomous agents that handle payables, RFP generation, vendor evaluation, proposal analysis, and contracting end-to-end. Unlike LLM wrappers, they run multi-step agentic workflows embedded into existing ERP stacks.

**Why interesting:** Procurement is a €10T+ global market drowning in manual workflows. Procure AI isn't selling dashboards — they're replacing whole workflows. 4× YoY revenue growth, 30% processing time reduction per customer, and €2.35M average savings per deployment are credible ROI signals. Live production with EnBW and Kärcher proves enterprise willingness to pay.

**Why Creandum fit:** This is Vesence's template applied to procurement — AI agents replacing high-touch professional workflows, sticky by nature, regulated enough to create moat. Founders (McKinsey, MIT/LBS) have the enterprise credibility to sell into procurement heads at DAX companies. Seed-stage at €13M means meaningful check size still available pre-Series A.

**Key risk:** Founded 2020 (6 years old) — not a fresh team. Why did it take until 2025 to find product-market fit? Mitigant: enterprise AI workflows needed GPT-4-class models to be viable; the 2023–2025 window was the unlock.

**Recommend:** Reach out to Konstantin von Büren directly.
