## Recommended First Outreach

### Scalera

**Why now:** Construction procurement remains fragmented, manual, and local, but AI can finally read specifications and map them into supplier/product catalogs. Scalera has the rare combination of a painful vertical workflow and concrete traction numbers.

**Question to validate:** Is the core wedge supplier-side tender automation, contractor-side procurement, or a two-sided network? The answer changes the moat.

**Sources:** https://www.scalera.ai/en, https://www.scalera.ai/en/investors

### Round Treasury

**Why now:** Finance teams are adopting AI first in workflow-heavy, data-rich areas where speed and controls matter. Round is close to Creandum's Rillet/Midas pattern but in treasury and finance automation.

**Question to validate:** How much of the $500M+ processed is recurring workflow volume versus one-off flows, and what is the net revenue model?

**Sources:** https://www.roundtreasury.com/post/round-raises-6m-to-build-the-ai-powered-finance-automation-platform-for-modern-finance-teams, https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/04/londons-round-treasury-raises-e5-1-million-to-build-ai-powered-finance-automation-platform-for-modern-finance-teams/

### Portia AI

**Why now:** Agentic workflows are moving from demos into production, and the missing layer is reliability, auth, permissions, auditability, and human-in-the-loop control.

**Question to validate:** Does the open-source SDK have genuine developer pull, or is the business still mostly founder-led enterprise interest?

**Sources:** https://tech.eu/2025/06/03/portia-ai-returning-to-a-large-company-would-be-a-devastating-failure/, https://uk.linkedin.com/company/portiaai

### FION Energy

**Why now:** Industrial batteries are becoming controllable software assets. FION's manufacturer-agnostic approach gives it a chance to own dispatch and optimization rather than just installation.

**Question to validate:** How much gross margin and defensibility live in software versus hardware/project delivery?

**Sources:** https://www.htgf.de/en/fion-energy-pre-seed-round/
